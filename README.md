# Hive Mind

Spin the comb, match the pollen, survive eight fields. A hex puzzle roguelike by Samderverse.

**Play:** https://samderverse.github.io/HiveMind/

Press where three cells meet, drag your finger round to turn them, and let go to lock them in. Make triangles of one colour to clear them, chain cascades for bigger multipliers, and spend nectar at the stall on charms that bend the rules. Five fields to beat, then Endless if you dare.

Music: "White Lie" by Harris Heller (StreamBeats); the boss music is the same track, warped live. Sound effects shared with Tilt by Samderverse.

Add it to your phone's home screen to play full screen and offline.
