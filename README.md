# Hive Mind

Spin the comb, match the pollen, survive eight fields. A hex puzzle roguelike by Samderverse.

**Play:** https://samderverse.github.io/HiveMind/

Press where three cells meet, drag your finger round to turn them, and let go to lock them in. Make triangles of one colour to clear them, chain cascades for bigger multipliers, and spend nectar in the shop on charms that bend the rules.

Add it to your phone's home screen to play full screen and offline.
